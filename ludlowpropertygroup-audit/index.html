Executive Summary

The situation in 60 seconds.

Ludlow Property Group's corporate site is the smallest asset in a portfolio that already wins at search: the facility brands rank, the corporate brand does not. The replacement site is built and staged. What remains is a clean cutover, and then pointing the new site at the one audience nobody else is serving: owners who want to sell their facility.

  1. Beyond the brand name, the site is invisible. 97.6% of organic traffic comes from the single query "ludlow property group." Nine of ten pages share one identical title, nine of ten have no H1, eight of ten have no meta description, and the homepage contains 41 words of crawlable text.
  2. The fix is already built, the cutover is the strategy. The staged Duda site repairs almost every on-page defect at once (856-word homepage, unique titles, real H1s and descriptions). Two regressions, a 301 map, legal-page preservation and a GA4 baseline are all that stand between here and flipping DNS.
  3. The deal-flow wedge is wide open. 103 seller-intent queries ("colorado self-storage facilities for sale", "sell my self storage facility") already show the site at positions 23-45 with zero clicks. The SERPs have exactly one national direct-buyer page (Store Space). Keyword difficulty is 2-3. The market prices a seller lead at a $16 CPC, and each converted lead is a six-figure-plus acquisition.
  4. Authority is near zero and the backlink profile is 91% spam, but the four real links prove the model: the Thesis Driven deep-dive alone produced both of the brand's AI citations. Disavow the junk, multiply the earned media.
  5. AI search already loves LPG, at the facility layer. Vail Airport Storage swept 4 of 4 models on its market prompt and GPT-5.5 recommended Montrose Self Storage unprompted. The corporate entity was cited 2 of 20 times, both via one article. The playbook that made the facilities visible now needs to run at corporate.

Our verdict

This is not a rescue, it is a launch. The live Squarespace site was a placeholder that ranked on brand alone; the Duda replacement is structurally sound and one week of migration hygiene away from shipping. The real opportunity is strategic: no owner-operator in the country has claimed the "sell your facility" search lane or the leisure-town narrative, and LPG owns both the data and the credibility to take them.

Cut over cleanly in 30 days. Ship the seller funnel and Colorado pages in 60 days. Publish the 1031/tax cluster and earn 8-10 industry links by 90 days, ahead of the Q4 seller season. We project 3-4x organic clicks by Q1 2027 and a repeatable pipeline of acquisition leads that no broker fee touches.

1
P0 · MIGRATION

The DNS cutover is the single biggest SEO action available.

live site: 9/10 duplicate titles · 9/10 no H1 · 8/10 no meta description · homepage 41 words

The live Squarespace site never had an SEO foundation. Nine of ten pages share the identical fallback title "Owners and operators of self storage properties in supply-constrained leisure markets," the only unique title (homepage) says "commercial properties" instead of self-storage, and hero headlines are H2s everywhere, the only H1 on the entire site is on the EULA page. The staged Duda site fixes every one of these defects in a single stroke: unique keyword-correct titles, data-rich descriptions, proper H1s, and a homepage with 856 words instead of 41.

Fix: Treat the cutover as this quarter's #1 SEO project. Complete the pre-flight checklist in Finding 2, point ludlowpropertygroup.com DNS at Duda, re-verify in Search Console, and submit the new sitemap the same day.
2
P0 · REGRESSION RISK

The migration could kill the site's best SEO asset if it ships as-is.

/portfolio: 5,786 impressions · pos 12.2 · 1,130 words + 44 facility links live vs 384 words + zero schema staged

The live portfolio page is the only page Google actually likes: 35 named properties, each with address, description and a deep link to its rental site. The staged Duda portfolio is visually superior but carries only 384 words of crawlable text, no per-property links, and no JSON-LD at all. Four more landmines: the live /terms page is the Kobe SMS/voice Terms of Service (10DLC compliance) and the EULA is a live compliance page, both must survive at resolvable URLs; the /cookie easter-egg page is indexed and in the sitemap; and the site has zero third-party analytics, so there will be no traffic baseline to judge the migration against.

Fix: Before DNS: restore per-property text and all 44 facility links on the Duda portfolio, build the 301 map for all 10 live URLs, keep /terms and the EULA resolvable, noindex or delete /cookie, and install GA4 on the live site today so a pre/post baseline exists. The staged multiscreensite.com URL is currently indexable, keep it noindexed until cutover to avoid duplicate-content dilution.
3
P1 · THE WEDGE

The seller deal-flow lane is empty, and the site already has a foot in it.

103 seller-intent queries · 719 impressions · pos 23-45 · 0 clicks · KD 2-3 · $16 CPC on "sell my self storage facility"

Owners searching "colorado self-storage facilities for sale" or "sell my self storage facility" find marketplaces (LoopNet, Crexi, BizBuySell) and exactly one national direct-buyer page (Store Space). No owner-operator competes. Meanwhile LPG's /portfolio already ranks 26-46 on four Colorado variants of the query, by accident. Keyword difficulty on the money terms is 2-3 out of 100, and Google Ads prices a "sell my self storage facility" click at $16, the market's own estimate of what a seller lead is worth. One converted lead is a six-figure-plus acquisition with no broker fee.

Fix: Ship a dedicated "Sell Your Facility" hub (direct-sale process, confidentiality, 39-property credibility, valuation explainer) plus a purpose-built "We buy self-storage in Colorado" page, both with FAQPage schema from the 30-question bank in Section 08. Publish within 30 days of cutover so the Q4 1031/tax seller wave lands on them.
4
P1 · AUTHORITY

Authority Score 7, and 91% of the backlink profile is literal spam.

112 referring domains · ~5 legitimate · 14+ domains on one Moldova IP · "buy backlinks online cheap" anchors

The profile splits into two worlds. The junk: spam-network blasts with naked-URL anchors, including domains literally anchored "buy backlinks online cheap premium pbn network." The gold: four editorial links, Thesis Driven (a full deep-dive on LPG's leisure-town thesis), Vail Daily, Realm Global and Pincus. Those four links punch far above their weight, the Thesis Driven piece alone produced both of the brand's AI citations in our 20-call test. Mechanical link-gap outreach is dead here: the computed 5-competitor link gap returned 21 domains, 100% of them spam networks.

Fix: File a disavow for the Moldova-IP and Singapore-directory clusters (1 hour). Then run the qualitative playbook: industry press bylines (Inside Self-Storage, Modern Storage Media, List Self Storage), state storage associations, the 10 facility-town chambers, and one Thesis-Driven-grade data story per quarter.
5
P1 · AI VISIBILITY

AI search cites the facilities constantly, and the corporate brand almost never.

corporate: 2 of 20 citations, both via one article · facility layer: Vail Airport Storage swept 4 of 4 models

On "Self storage operators in Vail Colorado," all four flagship models cited Vail Airport Storage, quoting its site, chamber listings, Facebook and YouTube. GPT-5.5 recommended Montrose Self Storage as its best-value Western Slope pick unprompted. The corporate entity surfaced only on "Who buys self storage facilities in small resort towns?", where Claude named Ludlow Property Group outright and Perplexity cited the Thesis Driven deep-dive. One earned article is carrying 100% of corporate AI visibility, single point of failure and proof of concept at once.

Fix: Publish the leisure-town thesis (with the data LLMs already quote: 6-year average tenant stay, 99% independent ownership, supply constraints) on the new site; add Organization schema linking the corporate entity to all facility brands via sameAs/subOrganization; pitch two more industry deep-dives. Re-run this 20-call test monthly.
