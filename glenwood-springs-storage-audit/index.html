02 · Executive Summary

The situation in 60 seconds.

The facility is 97% full, sells mostly vehicle-size enclosed drive-up units, and owns the only 14-foot-wide enclosed RV-length garages on the Hwy 82 corridor. Online, it barely exists.

  1. Until tonight the site was broken. A bad HTTPS certificate sent every visitor to a privacy error. We fixed it on November 11. There is still no Search Console, no analytics (both in progress), titles that never say "Glenwood Springs", and a false "Climate Controlled" headline.
  2. The Google listing is not ours yet. It is unclaimed, shows the previous owner's phone, has no website link, one photo, and a duplicate listing ("H Lazy F Self Storage") splits the reviews.
  3. AI assistants mention us 3 times in 20 answers, always as "Cavern Springs Storage" at the wrong street number. Our sister LPG brands were named 4 times, more than us.
  4. The market is small and weakly defended. About 140 searches a month for the whole "storage units glenwood springs" cluster. Directories hold all 10 organic spots, the map-pack leaders have 12 to 18 reviews, keyword difficulty is 0 to 23.
  5. With the facility already full, SEO here is about waitlist depth and pricing power on the vehicle units, and about owning the new brand name before the old names harden in Google and the AI models.

Our verdict

This is a fix-the-entity job, not a traffic-volume job. One name, one address, one phone, everywhere. Then a site that actually says Glenwood Springs, RV-length and drive-up in plain words.

Do the foundation in 14 days, the new pages and directory cleanup in 45, and the press, sponsorship and re-test by day 90.

What is moving the market

Three different systems decide who a Glenwood Springs customer finds. Each one rewards something different, so each needs its own fix. In plain language:

Classic SEO

Google organic results

  • Page 1 for every Glenwood storage search is 100% directories (U-Haul, Yelp, MapQuest, the Glenwood Chamber). No operator website ranks in the top 10 from Glenwood.
  • Competitors are weak: Citadel has an Authority Score of 20, everyone else 7 to 9.
  • The links that move rankings here are local: the Glenwood Chamber directory, Post Independent, Sopris Sun, a Summer of Music sponsorship (13 links to Citadel).
  • Our own Vail Airport and Snowmass Glenwood pages rank #9 to #18 for these searches and send people 35+ minutes away.
So: a real, fast page with "Glenwood Springs" in the title and H1 can enter the top 5.
Local SEO

The Google map pack

  • The 3-pack is decided by distance, listing completeness and reviews.
  • We already hold #3 for "boat storage glenwood springs" with no website link, 1 photo, one category and the old phone.
  • Nearest rivals: Royal Mini 0.3 km (4.9, 9 reviews), Thunder River 0.4 km (4.2, 16), Alpine (3.7, 12), Citadel (5.0, 18).
  • The "H Lazy F" duplicate and directory listings (StorageCafe, RentCafe, Cylex, Yelp) still carry old names, 5445 and the old phone.
So: claim and complete the listing, merge the duplicate, push to 25+ reviews.
AI / LLM search

ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity

  • No Google AI Overview appears on any Glenwood storage search today.
  • The assistants answer from directories (SpareFoot, SelfStorage.com, StorageCafe, BBB, Yelp) and from operators that publish price tables. Royal Mini is named in 13 of 20 answers because of its price list.
  • We are cited 3 of 20 times, all as "Cavern Springs", one with a false climate-control claim lifted from an old Yelp review.
So: one consistent identity everywhere, a "formerly Cavern Springs" statement, quotable facts (sizes, what fits, drive times), SpareFoot and SelfStorage.com listings.

01

HTTPS was broken on www.

Fixed · 2026-11-11
IMPACT: every visitor saw a privacy error · EFFORT: done

The hosting platform was serving its placeholder certificate (broken-ssl.multiscreensite.com), and the bare domain redirected everyone straight into that error. A storage customer on a phone would simply leave.

Done: a Let's Encrypt certificate is live (valid to 2027-02-09) and HTTPS is forced. Still to do: canonicals, og:url and the sitemap say http:// until the next publish, which the site cook handles.
02

The Google listing is unclaimed, outdated and duplicated.

Critical
IMPACT: map pack + AI answers · OWNER: Fishman · EFFORT: 45 min

The listing shows "Own this business?", the previous owner's phone (970) 331-0526, an "Add website" prompt and a single photo. A second listing, "H Lazy F Self Storage" at 5445 Co Rd 154, uses the same old phone and holds 3 more reviews. Google AI and the assistants read these listings first.

Fix: claim it, set the name, (970) 633-3122, the website, real hours and categories (Self-storage facility, RV storage facility, Boat storage facility), add 10+ real photos. Then have the H Lazy F listing marked as a duplicate, or claim it and close it.
03

No measurement at all.

In progress
IMPACT: we cannot see a single visit or rental click · EFFORT: ships with the cook

There was no Google Search Console property and no Google Analytics tag on any page, so nobody could see searches, clicks or waitlist sign-ups.

Status: a GA4 property with 6 key events (including rent and waitlist clicks) has been created, and the Search Console verification token is issued. Both go live with the site cook, then the sitemap gets submitted.
04

The pages never say "Glenwood Springs", and some say false things.

Critical
IMPACT: rankings + trust + AI accuracy · OWNER: site cook · EFFORT: 1 to 2 days
  • Titles are "Self Storage Units" and "Contact Us". Two H1s on the home page, zero H2s on the whole site, 153 words on the home page.
  • The hero says "Secure Units | Drive-Up | Climate Controlled". The units are not climate controlled.
  • Leftover template text: "self storage options in Gunter Texas"; a Size Guide with 5x5, 5x10 and "Parking" that are not sold here, while 10x25 and 14x35 are missing.
  • The FAQ points to a "Units and Pricing page" that does not exist and promises "24/7 surveillance" (cameras are still being installed).
  • The Terms page holds a privacy notice; schema includes a stray "Looking for Storage" organization and fake product codes.
Fix: one H1 per page and the new titles and metas in section 07, remove the climate claim, fix the Gunter Texas text, rebuild the Size Guide around the real sizes. Terms and Privacy go to legal review (Fishman), not an automatic rewrite.
05

AI assistants barely see us, and only under the dead name.

Critical
IMPACT: the fastest-growing discovery channel · EFFORT: weeks, mostly listings

3 of 20 answers mention us, all as "Cavern Springs Storage" at 5445. glenwoodspringsstorage.com was not retrieved once in 20 web searches. Meanwhile our sister pages (Vail Airport Storage, Snowmass Self Storage) capture Glenwood questions for facilities 35+ minutes away.

Fix: rename every directory listing, add "formerly Cavern Springs Storage" to the site and schema, publish quotable facts in plain text, and add a hand-off link from the Vail Airport and Snowmass Glenwood pages. Full detail in section 12.

11 · Competitive Landscape

Plenty of outdoor parking.
Almost no enclosed RV garages.

Thirty operators within about 25 miles. Most sell climate household storage or outdoor parking. Only a handful sell an enclosed garage long enough for an RV, and we are the closest one to town.

What we did, and what we deliberately did not do
  • Did: requested and installed the HTTPS certificate and turned on forced HTTPS (November 11). Created the GA4 property and issued the Search Console token.
  • Did not: change any price, or write any price into a page. Facility prices are owned by the automatic rate sync.
  • Did not: claim or edit the Google listings. That is Fishman's step with LPG's access.
  • Did not: rewrite the Terms or Privacy pages. Legal text goes to review.
  • Did not: claim cameras, 24/7 access or any amenity we could not verify.
Limitations and caveats
  • Drive times are estimates from road segments (Carbondale ~12 min, Silt ~30, Rifle ~38, Ruedi ~50). Verify in Google Maps before they go on a page.
  • Hours are unverified. The site, Google and older records disagree, and the rental system has none on file.
  • Market volumes are small. Local figures of 10 to 50 a month are rounded by Google and swing month to month; read them as direction, not precision.
  • Address: the Google pin and the rental system match 5455; some internal records and directories say 5445. This report standardizes on 5455 County Road 154.
  • AI answers are unstable. Repeat-prompt overlap is often under a third, so one run is a snapshot. Claude did not run a web search on prompt 2 despite the setting. Track the same 20 calls monthly.
  • Competitor prices are posted web rates seen in November 2026 and change often.
  • Lighthouse ran over http because HTTPS was broken at the time; re-run after the cook.
