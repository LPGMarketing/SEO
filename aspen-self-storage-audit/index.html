Executive Summary

The situation in 60 seconds.

Ludlow Property Group acquired the only storage facility in downtown Aspen in July 2026 and relaunched aspenselfstorage.com yesterday (Duda site 8b15d821, live since July 16). The building is nearly full: 11 indoor climate-controlled units, $333 to $950 per month, only 3 rentable and 8 waitlisted. The website, however, is a stock Cubby template that still belongs to someone else: another facility's photos, the old operator's schema and email, a false "Drive-Up" claim, and a title tag that just says "Self Storage Units". Meanwhile a near-namesake incumbent owns the local pack and the organic #1 for our own brand terms, and flagship AI models cited us 0 times in 20 probes while citing LPG's sister facility Snowmass Self Storage 11 times. The engineering is fine. Identity, truth, authority and measurement are the work.

  1. A name-collision incumbent owns our brand SERP. Aspen Self Storage Warehouses LLC (aspenselfstorageco.com, 211 Aspen Airport Business Center, est. 1979, 4.8 stars across 39 reviews) holds local pack #1 AND organic #1 for "aspen self storage". GPT-5.5 called it the "best pick"; Claude listed it first. LLMs even pulled aspenselfstorage.NET, a Pittsfield, New Hampshire facility, into Aspen CO price answers. The entity fight is the whole game.
  2. The site is telling the truth about someone else. The hero claims "Drive-Up" but every one of the 11 units is indoor and explicitly not drive-up. The photos are from Discount Self Storage (the sign is visible in one). The Organization schema says "Looking for Storage" and every page shows Help@LookingForStorage.com. Zero Ludlow mentions anywhere.
  3. Zero on-page identity. Homepage title "Self Storage Units" (18 characters, no Aspen, no brand), Duda site_seo title and description are null, no H2s anywhere on the site, 219 words of homepage text, no FAQPage schema, no og:image, LocalBusiness schema disabled in Duda.
  4. The backlink profile is 100% spam. 192 backlinks from 104 referring domains: URL shorteners, scraper directories, and an active PBN-seller cluster (backlinkhouse / backlinkon / backlinkshouse / backlinkstree, same Singapore IP) with anchors advertising "buy backlinks online cheap". Real authority: zero. Disavow, then build real citations.
  5. The playbook is proven in-house. Snowmass Self Storage, LPG's sister facility 10 minutes away, earned 11 of 20 citations in the same test and is the price authority LLMs quote for "storage near Aspen". The owner directive is a two-brand moat: Cooper owns downtown, climate, ski and luggage; Snowmass owns drive-up, RV, 24/7 and value. Cannibalization between them is desired; competitors must now beat two brands.

Our verdict

This is the strongest real-world position we have audited (the only downtown facility, nearly full, in a market where no competitor publishes prices) attached to the weakest possible digital identity: a template that misdescribes the building, credits the old operator, and has never been indexed. Nothing here is hard to fix, but all of it is urgent because the incumbent absorbs every search we do not answer.

Fix identity and truth in 30 days (title, photos, claims, schema, GBP, GA4/GSC, disavow). Ship the money pages in 60 days (pricing transparency, ski-gear wedge, corridor pages). Compound authority and re-run the AI probe by day 90. Target: local pack top-3, first LLM citations, and a waitlist that keeps deepening at higher rates.

02 · The Five Critical Findings

What's actually
holding the site back.

Each finding comes from the live crawl, the live SERPs, the backlink data, or the 20-call AI test, not from theory. Performance is not on this list: Lighthouse desktop scored 91 / 89 / 100 / 92. This is a content, authority and entity problem, not an engineering one.

· · ·
1
CRITICAL · ENTITY / BRAND

A name-collision incumbent owns the local pack and the organic #1 for our own brand.

Impact: Every "aspen self storage" search, branded or not, currently resolves to a competitor 4 miles away, in Maps, in organic, and in AI answers.

Aspen Self Storage Warehouses LLC (aspenselfstorageco.com, 211 Aspen Airport Business Center, established 1979, 4.8 stars across 39 reviews) holds local pack #1 and organic #1 on both live Aspen SERPs we pulled. In the flagship AI test, GPT-5.5 named it the "best pick" and Claude listed it first. The confusion is already cross-state: GPT-5.5 quoted prices from aspenselfstorage.net, a facility in Pittsfield, New Hampshire, inside an Aspen, Colorado cost answer, and Sonar pulled Aspen-branded facilities in Flagstaff, AZ and Fort Smith, AR into its sources. Google and the LLMs currently cannot tell the entities apart, and the incumbent wins every tie.

Fix: Aggressive entity disambiguation. Brand every surface "Aspen Self Storage on Cooper" with the full 630 East Cooper Avenue NAP: title tags, H1s, GBP, schema (with geo coordinates and sameAs), citations, and press. Verify who owns aspenselfstorageco.com; if LPG somehow controls it, 301 it here, otherwise treat it as competitor #1. Eta: naming half a day, then everywhere forever.
2
CRITICAL · TRUTH / TRUST

The site makes false claims with another facility's photos under the old operator's brand.

Impact: Legal and trust exposure, plus Google and AI engines learning wrong facts about the facility from its own website.

The hero banner promises "Drive-Up", yet every one of the 11 units is described in the rental widget as "Climate controlled, Indoor, Storage only, Not drive-up". The About page claims outdoor RV/boat/vehicle parking and "wide drive aisles" that do not exist. The photo set is from a different facility entirely (filenames say "facility-broadway"; one image visibly shows a DISCOUNT SELF STORAGE sign; the hero shows outdoor orange drive-up doors while this building is 100% indoor). And the site still legally identifies as the previous operator: Organization schema "Looking for Storage" on every page, contact email Help@LookingForStorage.com, zero mentions of Ludlow Property Group.

Fix: A one-day truth pass: kill the Drive-Up claim and lead with "Indoor Climate-Controlled", rewrite the About copy to the real building, shoot or source real facility photos plus licensed Aspen scenery, replace the Organization schema with Ludlow Property Group, and swap the old email for an LPG-controlled address. Eta: 1 day copy + photo swap.
3
CRITICAL · ON-PAGE IDENTITY

Zero on-page identity: an 18-character title, null SEO fields, no H2s, 219 words.

Impact: Google has almost nothing to rank and AI engines have almost nothing to quote. The site cannot win searches it never enters.

The homepage title tag is Self Storage Units: 18 characters, no "Aspen", no brand, no address. The meta description is Cubby boilerplate ("near you"). Duda's own site_seo title and description fields are null, and Duda's LocalBusiness schema toggle is disabled, so the only structured data lives inside the Cubby widget's shadow DOM (where crawlers may never execute it) with an empty openingHoursSpecification. There are no H2 headings anywhere on the site, no FAQPage schema, no og:image on any page, 4 of 5 homepage images lack alt text, and the homepage carries just 219 words of parseable text (a 1% content rate).

Fix: Rewrite titles and metas for all 9 URLs (table in Quick Wins), fill Duda site_seo, enable LocalBusiness/SelfStorage schema in the main document with geo and hours, add a real heading hierarchy and og:image, and grow the homepage to 600+ words of true, local, quotable copy. Eta: 1 to 2 days.
4
HIGH · AUTHORITY / TOXICITY

The backlink profile is a toxic shell: 100% spam, including a live PBN-seller cluster.

Impact: Zero usable link equity, plus an active spam footprint (avg spam score 58) attached to the domain during its most fragile indexing window.

Semrush sees 192 backlinks from 104 referring domains; DataForSEO sees 46 from 41. Both agree on the composition: essentially 100% junk. The top referrer is a URL shortener, the rest are scraper directories, .info/.pro/.eu/.fyi link-network pages (rankvance and friends), and an active PBN-seller cluster: backlinkhouse / backlinkon / backlinkshouse / backlinkstree, all resolving to the same Singapore IP, placed May and June 2026, with six anchors literally advertising "premium PBN network... buy backlinks online cheap". Zero editorial links. Zero Colorado links. Zero local citations.

Fix: File a disavow for the PBN cluster and the shortener/scraper networks (Tier 0 of the Backlink Strategy), then replace the profile with real citations: GBP, the LPG corporate portfolio page, the five aggregators LLMs already quote, and the Aspen Chamber. Eta: disavow 2 hours; citations week 1-2.
5
HIGH · MEASUREMENT

No measurement stack: the only analytics on the site belong to Cubby.

Impact: LPG cannot see traffic, rankings, conversions or waitlist signups. Every dollar of the work above would be unattributable.

The single tag firing on the site is GA4 property G-2SVMV22G2M, configured with Cubby's storefront ID (d0f2c17f...), pushing CubbyFacility dataLayer events to Cubby, not to LPG. There is no owner GA4, no Google Tag Manager, no Search Console verification meta, no Meta pixel, no call tracking on the site. Because the facility is nearly full, the KPIs that matter (waitlist signups, rate power, overflow routed to Snowmass) are exactly the kind of conversions only a properly-owned analytics stack can capture.

Fix: Create an LPG-owned GA4 property and add it via Duda, verify GSC (DNS or meta), configure waitlist-signup and checkout events, and stand up a Semrush position-tracking project for the keyword map in Section 05. Eta: half a day. The zero baseline becomes an asset the moment this ships.
Bottom line: the building is the moat and the website is the leak. A near-full, downtown-monopoly facility is being represented online by a template that names the wrong operator, shows the wrong building, claims the wrong amenities, and has never been indexed. Fix identity, truth, authority and measurement, in that order, and the market has no answer to 630 East Cooper.

EXHIBIT · CROSS-STATE ENTITY CONFUSION

GPT-5.5 answered "storage cost in Aspen" with prices from New Hampshire.

In the cost prompt, GPT-5.5 cited aspenselfstorage.NET, a facility in Pittsfield, New Hampshire, and presented its cheap drive-up rates ($110 for a 10x10, $150 for a 10x20) as Aspen, Colorado pricing. Sonar's sources for the same question included Aspen-branded facilities in Flagstaff, Arizona and Fort Smith, Arkansas. Three near-identical domains (.com us, .co the incumbent, .net New Hampshire) are being conflated by the engines in live answers today.

This is why the fix is entity-level, not keyword-level: strong geo schema (39.1880, -106.8175), the "on Cooper" brand suffix on every surface, exact-NAP citations, and sameAs links that let a model verify which Aspen it is quoting. Whoever publishes the clearest machine-readable identity wins the collision, and today nobody has.

Why this matters now

AI assistants are already the choice-influencer for exactly this facility's customer: affluent, digital-first, often planning from out of state. Every probe returned real operators with real prices, and the answers are being assembled from a small, fixable set of sources. Being absent from 20 of 20 answers while nearly full is fine today; being absent in November, when ski-season demand and the waitlist economics peak, would be leaving rate power on the table.

What we deliberately did NOT do
  • We did not analyze GSC or GA4 history, because none exists: neither was ever connected, and the only tag on the site is Cubby's own storefront GA4. The zero baseline is documented in Section 01 as a clean-slate advantage.
  • We did not run mobile Lighthouse: the tool exposes desktop only. Scores cited (91 / 89 / 100 / 92) are desktop lab runs.
  • We did not use DataForSEO's LLM-mentions database, which is empty for every keyword in this market (too small); AI visibility was measured with live probes instead.
  • We did not deploy any change to the live site; every recommendation is scoped and ready.
  • We did not claim or modify the Google Business Profile, and the disavow file is drafted as a recommendation, not yet filed.
  • We did not buy or place any links.
Limitations & caveats
  • The LLM test is a 20-call sample; individual model answers vary run to run. Re-run monthly with the identical prompts so the trend is comparable.
  • Aspen niche volumes (ski, luggage, wine, business) sit near or below tracking thresholds; treat them as directional, validated by SERP evidence and forum demand rather than volume databases.
  • Ownership of aspenselfstorageco.com is unverified; the audit treats it as competitor #1 pending confirmation. If LPG controls it, the correct move is a 301, not a fight.
  • Review counts and local-pack composition shift continuously; figures captured July 17, 2026.
  • Semrush trends reports are plan-gated and no Semrush site-audit project exists for this domain yet; create one post-relaunch.
