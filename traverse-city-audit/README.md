# Traverse City Storage, SEO Audit (July 2026)

Technical, content, competitive and local-visibility audit of **traversecitystorage.com**,
a four-location self storage brand in Traverse City, Michigan.

**Live report:** https://lpgmarketing.github.io/SEO/traverse-city-audit/

## Headline

The site launched 29 June 2026 as an unedited template. All four location pages returned
404 from every internal link, not one page named the city, and the site carried no
LocalBusiness schema. The brand also has **no Google Business Profile at all**, in a market
whose local pack is held by a listing with 3.9 stars and 17 reviews.

The site-side issues were fixed during the audit and verified live. What remains needs the
GBP login, a photo shoot, and a review drive.

## Method

DataForSEO Business Listings (two sweeps), live Traverse City SERPs, Google Ads volume,
live HTTP and rendered-DOM crawls of every URL, and a read-only walk of the Cubby widget.
No GSC or GA4 data exists for this property yet, which is itself a P0 finding.

Prepared by ThinkFlowLabs for Ludlow Property Group. Confidential, noindex.
