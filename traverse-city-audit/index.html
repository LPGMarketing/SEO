Executive Summary

The situation in 60 seconds.

Traverse City Storage runs four facilities on the northeast side of Traverse City, Michigan, and went live on 29 June 2026 as a Duda site (1052fb71) that was never edited past the template stage. This is not a ranking problem. It is a completeness problem, and that is good news, because completeness is cheap to fix.

Before this audit: every internal link to a location page was broken, the homepage title was the literal string "Self Storage Units", all four facility pages shared the identical title "Self Storage", the content library still held (123) 123-1234 and EMAIL@EMAIL.COM, and the site emitted no LocalBusiness markup of any kind. The header call button displayed an Illinois phone number while its link dialled a Michigan one.

The commercial context makes this urgent rather than merely untidy. The head-term cluster runs 320 searches a month at an $11.79 cost per click, roughly $3,800 a month of equivalent paid traffic, and the local pack that captures most of it is held by a listing with 3.9 stars and 17 reviews. Second place is held on eight reviews. This is the softest storage pack in the LPG portfolio, and we are not competing in it, because we have no listing to compete with.

  • Fixed today: 4 broken location pages, 10 page titles, sitewide schema, placeholder contact data, a wrong phone number
  • Biggest lever: claim the unclaimed predecessor listing sitting 66 metres from our own gate, with its 16 reviews intact
  • Blocking dependency: there is no facility photography, which stalls both the hero rebuild and the GBP photo plan
  • Measurement gap: not in the property registry, so no GSC, no GA4, nothing measurable
The clean-slate advantage: the domain is one month old, so there are no legacy rankings to protect, no penalties to inherit, and no cannibalisation to untangle. Every fix compounds from a true zero. The flip side is that there is also no Search Console or Analytics baseline, because the property was never registered. Wiring those is step one of being able to prove any of this worked.

Critical findings

What's actually
holding the site back.

· · ·
1
CRITICAL · FIXED TODAY

Every location page returned a 404, from every internal link.

Impact: on a four-location brand, the entire local surface was unreachable by click.

The /facilities page rendered its links as traverse-city-storage---garfield with a triple dash, while the live page and the sitemap both use traverse-city-storage-garfield with a single one. Anyone browsing to a location hit a dead end. Google could still crawl the pages because the sitemap carried the working slugs, but no human could reach them.

Root cause is upstream. The facility/~page-item~ dynamic page binds to an external collection whose page_item_url values disagree with Duda's own slug normalisation. Four 301 redirects were shipped as a patch and verified live. The feed itself should be corrected in Cubby so the redirects can eventually be retired.

2
CRITICAL · NEEDS THE CLIENT

The brand has no Google Business Profile, and the predecessor listing holding 16 reviews is unclaimed 66 metres away.

Impact: invisible in the local pack, which is where most storage clicks in this market are decided.

Two independent DataForSEO sweeps (a 15 km title search and a 3 km category search around the facility cluster, both including unclaimed listings) returned nothing at any of the four addresses. The brand is not losing a ranking contest, it is not in the index.

The opening is unusually good. Colonial Self Storage LLC at 1057 W South Airport Rd carries 3.8 stars across 16 reviews and is unclaimed. Two facts tie it to us: its phone number, (231) 929-8181, was already sitting in our own Duda content library, and its map pin is 66 metres from the N Garfield gate. Claiming and renaming it preserves the CID and the reviews. Starting a fresh listing throws away 16 reviews in a market where 17 wins the pack.

3
HIGH · FIXED TODAY

Not one page named Traverse City, and the site emitted no LocalBusiness markup at all.

Impact: a local business that told neither users nor search engines where it operates.

The homepage title was the generic string "Self Storage Units". All ten pages have been rewritten to name the city, phrased from the winning query forms in the volume data. Separately, the entire site emitted a single bare WebSite node: no address, no coordinates, no hours, no entity. An Organization plus four SelfStorage nodes with full postal addresses, geo coordinates, separate office and gate hours, and amenity features now ship on five page types.

Deliberately omitted: sameAs and hasMap pointing at a GBP CID, because no listing exists yet, and priceRange, because the Cubby widget returns no rentable units and any price would be invented. Both go in the moment those exist.

4
HIGH · WORKED AROUND

The dynamic page's title cannot be set through the Duda API.

Impact: four location pages shared one title, and the obvious fix silently did nothing.

Setting the page-level SEO title on the dynamic facility page had no effect whatsoever. The live title stayed "Self Storage" on all four because the external collection's G.seo-title field wins. This was caught only by verifying the live page after publishing, not by trusting the API's success response.

Worked around with a header block that maps each facility label to a unique, street-level title and meta description. Verified live on all four. The durable fix belongs upstream in the feed.

5
HIGH · BLOCKS THE REBUILD

Every image is stock, the logo is a temp file, and the copy contradicts itself.

Impact: blocks the family hero rebuild and the entire GBP photo plan.

The homepage runs stock-interior-01, stock-facility-exterior and two more stock files, and the logo is still 650x250-logo-temp.png. The LPG family hero is built around a real facility photograph behind a gradient scrim, so it cannot be installed honestly over stock imagery.

Two truth problems sit alongside it. The facility pages post gate access as 6 AM to 10 PM while the section directly below claims 24/7 access. And the header call button displayed (618)-384-0023, an Illinois number, while its link dialled the correct Michigan one. The phone bug was fixed today. The hours contradiction needs an operations answer, not an SEO one.

Our verdict

This is a strong physical position attached to an unfinished digital one. Four facilities inside four kilometres of downtown, in a market whose local pack is held by a 3.9-star listing and whose second slot is held on eight reviews, and we were not in the running because the brand had no listing and no reachable location pages.

Nothing in this report is technically hard. The site-side blockers were fixed in a single session. What remains is mostly physical and administrative: claim a listing we already own, photograph four buildings, and ask real customers for reviews. Those three things, done in the next 30 days, put us in the pack.

Targets: local pack presence for the head term by week 8, 18 or more reviews at 4.5+ by week 12, organic page one for self storage traverse city by week 12.

Methodology

Method &
limitations.

Sources. DataForSEO Business Listings (two sweeps: 15 km title search and 3 km category search around the facility cluster, both with unclaimed listings included), DataForSEO live SERP for the Traverse City geo, Google Ads search volume for the Traverse City location, live HTTP status checks on every URL, live rendered-DOM crawls via Playwright at 1440 width, a read-only walk of the Cubby widget's nested shadow roots, and the Duda Partner API for site configuration.

What we could not measure. There is no Google Search Console or Analytics data in this report, and that is not an oversight. The property is absent from the LPG property registry, so no Search Console property was ever registered and the service account has no access. The domain is also one month old, so meaningful data would be thin regardless. Semrush was not pulled for the same reason. Every volume figure here is third-party estimated demand, not our measured traffic. Fixing this is a P0 in the 30-day plan precisely because none of the targets above are provable until it is done.

Caveats worth stating. Distances are straight-line from the downtown centroid, not drive-time. West Bay's coordinates were not returned by the API, so its distance is approximate from its Cherry Bend Road address. Google's crawl lags: National Storage showed 165 reviews in one call and 169 in the live pack the same day, and we cite the live figure. Maps and local results are personalised, so ranks vary by user and device. Review velocity was not sampled because we have no listing of our own to sample. Amenity claims in the schema were taken only from the facility pages' own published feature list, never from keyword demand or competitor listings.
