02 · Executive Summary

The situation in 60 seconds.

Estes Park Storage is an inversion case. The hard parts of local SEO, the organic rankings and the AI answer, are already won. The easy parts, the map pack, conversion tracking, and basic site surface area, are where it loses.

  1. The wins are banked. #1 organic on every core head term ("estes park storage units", "estes park self storage", "storage units estes park co"), cited in 14 of 20 flagship LLM calls with web search on (Perplexity: 5 for 5, all effectively #1), and ranked #1 inside 9 of those 14 answers.
  2. The map is inverted. In the local pack for "storage units estes park co" the site sits #3 behind Estes Valley Self Storage (4.0 stars, 4 reviews) and Woodstock Self Storage (5.0, 7 reviews), despite holding 4.6 stars across 55 reviews, roughly 10× the review count of either. That is a GBP relevance and category problem, not a reputation problem.
  3. Conversion tracking is dead. 1 key event in 90 days across ~744 GA4 sessions, with two GA4 tags (G-2SVMV22G2M + G-L18W0K7JF5) firing on every page. The business cannot see which channel rents units.
  4. It is a 4-page site wearing a 12-page market. Only 4 pages rank at all. There is no RV / boat / winter vehicle page (the local pack is won, the organic result is lost to Alpine), no pricing page (national questions worth 18,100 + 5,400 + 2,900 searches/mo), and no corridor pages for Lyons, Glen Haven, Drake or Allenspark, where nobody ranks.
  5. The entity needs hygiene. A legacy identity ("IN Self Storage") still leaks: Yelp links to inselfstorage.com, Gemini cites a wrong address (1580 Fish Creek Rd), a stray "Looking for Storage" Organization schema ships on every page, and 72% of referring domains are link-farm spam awaiting disavow.

Our verdict

Estes Park Storage owns the organic SERP and the AI answer, but loses the map pack it deserves to win, and its website is a 4-page site wearing a 12-page market.

Fix the GBP and the tracking in 2 weeks. Ship the 8 missing pages in 6 weeks. Clean the entity and the link profile in 90 days. The market is small (~630 head-term searches/mo) but uncontested: no REIT operates in town, and no competitor publishes real content. Every move in this report is winnable.

1
CRITICAL · LOCAL

The local pack is inverted: #1 organic, #3 on the map, behind facilities with 4 and 7 reviews.

Impact: the highest-intent surface in local search ranks our weakest. Pack order on "storage units estes park co": Estes Valley (4.0, 4 reviews), Woodstock (5.0, 7), us (4.6, 55).

The site is #1 organic for the entire money cluster, and the GBP holds 4.6 stars across 55 reviews, roughly 10× the review count of either facility above it. When the review leader loses the pack, the problem is relevance, not reputation: primary category, services, products, photo freshness, and posting cadence are the levers Google is reading.

One more thing to rule out: the facility sits at 1210 Woodstock Dr, and "Woodstock Self Storage" is a separate, verified competitor on the same street. That same-street name collision can split relevance signals between the two entities.

Fix: Full GBP audit: confirm primary category "Self-storage facility", fill every services and products field (unit sizes, vehicle/RV parking, online rental), upload fresh photos, start a 4-6 week post cadence, and mirror the top FAQ answers into GBP Q&A. Verify the address formatting matches the site and schema exactly so Woodstock Dr addressing isn't splitting relevance with the same-street competitor. Eta: half a day. This is the single highest-leverage move in the report.
2
CRITICAL · ANALYTICS

Conversion tracking is dead, and two GA4 tags are firing on every page.

Impact: 1 key event across ~744 sessions in 90 days. Rental attribution is blind; every marketing decision is a guess.

GA4 property 505027139 recorded exactly one key event (a purchase) in 90 days. Organic Search delivered 372 sessions and 1 key event; Direct 311 and 0. Meanwhile the Playwright crawl found two GA4 measurement IDs firing through GTM container GTM-NH83G4GK: G-2SVMV22G2M and G-L18W0K7JF5. One is likely a stale duplicate double-counting sessions, and the events that matter (Cubby checkout steps, click-to-call) are not marked as key events at all.

Fix: Consolidate to a single GA4 property, mark purchase, begin_checkout and click-to-call as key events, and verify the Cubby widget's dataLayer events flow into GA4. Eta: 2 hours. Until this ships, nothing else in this report can be measured.
3
HIGH · ON-PAGE

The homepage title is "Self Storage Units", and the FAQ page renders zero answers.

Impact: the most valuable tag on the site is boilerplate, and the FAQ page claims 8 sections of answers in schema that never display.

The homepage <title> is literally "Self Storage Units": no brand, no "Estes Park", and og:title / twitter:title inherit it. Most page titles repeat the pattern ("Contact Us", "Blog", "Size Guide").

On /faq, all 8 accordion widgets fail to initialize (8× "ssr initiate widget - element not found" console errors), so visitors see section headings with no answers, while FAQPage JSON-LD still claims the full Q&A. That is a UX dead-end plus a schema/content mismatch risk in one page.

Fix: Ship the 8-page title/meta rewrite set in Section 05 (15 minutes for the homepage alone). Rebuild the FAQ as static, crawlable text and regenerate FAQPage schema from what actually renders. Eta: 2 hours.
4
HIGH · CONTENT

A 4-page site in a 12-page market.

Impact: 36 ranked keywords vs a market with 18,100/mo of unanswered national questions and a wide-open RV/vehicle cluster.

Only 4 pages rank at all (home, about, contact, tour-photos), and the homepage carries 100% of organic traffic. The misses are specific: no RV/boat/vehicle page, so the site holds local pack #1 for "rv storage estes park" but loses the organic #1 to Alpine, whose homepage title is literally "RV, Vehicle & Self-Storage Facility in Estes Park CO". No pricing or size pages, while "how much is a storage unit" (18,100/mo), "how much does a storage unit cost" (5,400/mo) and "what size storage unit do i need" (2,900/mo) go unanswered locally, and aggregators render "$Infinity" placeholders for Estes Park. No corridor pages for Lyons, Glen Haven, Drake or Allenspark, where nobody ranks. And /tour-photos ships with no H1 and no meta description.

Fix: Ship the 10-piece content roadmap in Section 09, pricing guide first, RV page second, winter wedge by mid-August. Eight of the ten are pages, not posts: this is surface area, not blogging.
5
HIGH · ENTITY

Entity and link hygiene: the old name still leaks, and 72% of referring domains are spam.

Impact: Gemini already cites a wrong address for the facility; Google's entity graph still contains the legacy brand.

The facility used to operate as "IN Self Storage", and the residue is everywhere: Yelp's website link points to inselfstorage.com, the query "in self storage estes park" still pulls 142 impressions in GSC, and a stray Organization schema named "Looking for Storage" ships on every page of the site. In the LLM test, Gemini cited the facility at 1580 Fish Creek Rd, a conflation with the Fish Creek / Lakeview entity, the only hard NAP error in 20 calls.

The backlink profile compounds it: 86 backlinks from 39 referring domains, but roughly 28 of 39 domains are spam (Moldova-hosted link farms on shared IP 195.20.19.178, fiverr ".site" testimonial pages), only 10 links follow, and the single real local editorial link is estesparknews.com.

Fix: Remove the "Looking for Storage" schema block, fix the Yelp website link, claim and standardize every legacy-name citation to "Estes Park Storage, 1210 Woodstock Dr, (970) 586-6811", and file a disavow for the Moldova/fiverr farms. Eta: ~4 hours total. This also directly attacks the wrong-address AI citations.
Bottom line: Findings 1, 2, 3 and 5 are fixable inside two weeks with no new content. Finding 4 is six weeks of page building into uncontested SERPs. Nothing here requires more reviews, more budget, or a redesign: the site already won the hard part.
