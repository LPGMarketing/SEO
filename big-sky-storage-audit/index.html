Executive Summary

The situation in 60 seconds.

Big Sky Storage has the only heated and climate-controlled product in the Gallatin Canyon, real press coverage and a well-written site. Google can barely see any of it, for reasons that are almost entirely mechanical and cheap to fix. AI assistants, oddly, already can.

  1. Google has indexed 3 of 18 pages. The seven pages that sell the product (storage units, heated storage, RV and boat, sizes, heated vs climate) are "Discovered, currently not indexed" or unknown to Google. The homepage links to none of them; they hang off /blog, which is not indexed either.
  2. The Google Maps listing is the wrong business shape. A listing at 204 Blacksmith Lp already wins the local pack for "big sky storage", but it has no website, no phone, is categorized as a Warehouse and shows as Closed.
  3. Every page tells Google this business is "Looking for Storage". The sitewide Organization schema, the schema email and the footer all carry Cubby's parent brand and Help@LookingForStorage.com. A GPT-4o scoping probe asked about bigskystorage.com answered that it could not find a website and offered a different "Storage-Big Sky" at 47995 Gallatin Rd; the flagship models do better (Section 10), but the Belgrade name-alike still appears in 5 of their 20 answers.
  4. The brand rankings are stuck on the old domain. storagebigsky.com still ranks #2 for "big sky storage" in Semrush; the new domain ranks for 2 keywords. The bare https://bigskystorage.com still serves Duda's placeholder certificate.
  5. The demand window is now. "rv storage bozeman" runs 140 searches a month on average and 390 in October; "car storage bozeman" 110 and 320. The facility opens into the fall vehicle swap, the second-home arrival season and the November 25 resort opening.

Our verdict

This is not a content problem and not an authority problem yet. It is a discoverability problem: unlinked pages, a half-built Maps listing, the wrong entity name in the code and a broken bare domain. Most of it is a day of Duda and Business Profile work.

Fix the foundation before October 16. Earn 10 reviews and 10 real local links in 60 days. Target the Bozeman vehicle-storage terms with honest drive-time pages in 90 days. AI assistants already cite us in 13 of 20 flagship answers; the realistic goal is to match that in Maps and organic by the end of ski season's first month.

02 · The Five Critical Findings

What's actually
holding the site back.

Each one was observed on the live site, the live SERP or the live API on October 5. Each has a fix that fits inside the eleven days before opening.

· · ·
1
CRITICAL · INDEXING

Google has indexed 3 of 18 sitemap URLs, and none of the pages that sell.

Impact: the heated, RV and storage-units pages cannot rank for anything. Every non-brand query falls back to the homepage.

URL Inspection API, Oct 5: indexed are /, /reserve-before-big-sky-storage-opens and /how-far-big-sky-storage-from-town-center-bozeman. Twelve URLs are "Discovered, currently not indexed", including /storage-units-big-sky-mt, /heated-storage-big-sky-mt and /rv-boat-trailer-storage-big-sky-mt. Three are unknown to Google: /what-size-storage-unit-big-sky-mt, /tenant-protection, /terms-and-conditions.

The cause is structural. The homepage's internal links go to about, blog, contact, faq, photo-gallery, size-guide, tenant-protection, privacy and terms. None of the seven landing pages are linked from the homepage or the main nav. They live as Duda blog posts, reachable only through /blog, which is itself not indexed. To Google, they are orphans on a brand-new, low-authority domain.

Fix: Add a "Storage at Big Sky" block to the homepage and footer linking all seven landing pages with descriptive anchors ("heated storage in Big Sky", "RV, boat and trailer storage"). Add the three product pages to the main nav under a Storage menu. Then request indexing for each in the GSC URL Inspection tool (manual, 10 a day), and submit to Bing Webmaster Tools with IndexNow. Eta: half a day.
2
CRITICAL · LOCAL

The Maps listing wins the pack, with no website, no phone and the wrong category.

Impact: the single highest-converting surface for a storage facility sends nobody to the site and gives nobody a number to call.

DataForSEO's live local pack shows a "Big Sky Storage" listing at 204 Blacksmith Lp in position #1 for "big sky storage" and #3 for "heated storage big sky", carrying no website and no phone. ChatGPT's map card shows the same place as "Closed · Warehouse". It is absent from the pack for "storage units big sky mt", which belongs to Lazy J, Big Sky Warehouses and Canyon Storage. This is the listing everything else (reviews, AI answers, the Google Ads location asset) will hang off.

Fix: First check the LPG Business Profile manager and Zernio for an existing claim, since filing a duplicate on an unclaimed or pending place triggers suspension. Then claim the existing place, set primary category Self storage facility, add https://www.bigskystorage.com/?utm_source=GBP, (406) 623-9410, opening date Oct 16, access and office hours, the real construction photos and the services list. Link it to Google Ads account 8099839192. Eta: 1 hour plus verification time.
3
CRITICAL · ENTITY

Every page tells Google the business is called "Looking for Storage".

Impact: Google and AI assistants cannot resolve a single clean entity for Big Sky Storage, so they substitute name-alikes.

Sitewide JSON-LD ships {"@type":"Organization","name":"Looking for Storage","image":null}, injected from Duda's business info. The homepage's otherwise good SelfStorage node lists "email":"Help@LookingForStorage.com", and the same address appears 6 times in the homepage HTML and 18 times on /about-us, visible in the footer. A SearchAction points to /search?q=, a page that does not exist.

The name space is already crowded: Big Sky Self Storage (Belgrade, ranks #1 for "big sky self storage"), a Big Sky Storage in Helena, and a "Storage-Big Sky" at 47995 Gallatin Rd #201 with phone (406) 995-4296. Asked about bigskystorage.com, a GPT-4o scoping probe replied that it could not find a website and offered that one instead. In the flagship test, Claude split us into two businesses and the Belgrade name-alike appeared in 5 of 20 answers.

Fix: In Duda business info set the name to Big Sky Storage and a Big Sky email address (Fishman or ops to choose the inbox). Remove the sitewide SearchAction. Give the SelfStorage node a stable @id and reference it from every page. Add a disambiguation FAQ: "Is Big Sky Storage the same as Big Sky Self Storage in Belgrade?" The Cubby footer email is an org-scoped Cubby setting; change it only after confirming scope, since it is shared across LPG facilities. Eta: 2 hours.
4
CRITICAL · DOMAIN

The brand rankings are stuck on the old domain, and the bare domain is broken.

Impact: the only equity the brand has is on a domain we are retiring, and anyone typing bigskystorage.com with https sees a security warning.

Semrush: storagebigsky.com (Authority Score 7) still ranks #2 for "big sky storage" (140/mo) and #3 for "storage units big sky mt" (110/mo). It 301s correctly to https://www.bigskystorage.com/, but the new domain (AS 2) holds only 2 keywords. Brave's index still shows the old domain. Separately, https://bigskystorage.com/ serves Duda's placeholder certificate CN=broken-ssl.multiscreensite.com and fails with SEC_E_UNTRUSTED_ROOT; http:// apex and www are fine. This has been open since at least Sep 8.

Fix: In Duda's domain settings, add the bare domain as a connected domain and reissue SSL so Duda's certificate covers both hostnames (apex A record must point at Duda). Verify storagebigsky.com in Search Console and file a Change of Address to bigskystorage.com, which transfers signals much faster than a 301 alone. Keep the 301 in place permanently. Eta: 1 hour, DNS owner (Fishman) required.
5
CRITICAL · CORE WEB VITALS

Mobile performance is 20. The heated-storage page's LCP is 22 seconds.

Impact: half of our GSC impressions (180 of 360) are mobile; a 12-second homepage loses them before the hero renders.

DataForSEO Lighthouse (mobile, Oct 5): homepage Performance 20, Accessibility 93, Best Practices 77, SEO 100; LCP 12.0 s, CLS 0.794, TBT 1,050 ms. /heated-storage-big-sky-mt: Performance 51, LCP 22.4 s, caused by a 2.1 MB PNG hero. Desktop crawl measured CLS 0.386 on the homepage, one shift at 198 ms on block #1716942098, down from 0.456 on Sep 11 but still failing (good is under 0.1). The Cubby widget is ~7,000 px and lazy-loads below.

Fix: Re-export every hero and band image as WebP at 1600 px wide (target under 250 KB). Give the hero image explicit width and height and fetchpriority="high". Reserve height for the block that shifts. Move the Cubby widget below a fixed-height placeholder. Re-test until mobile Performance is 60+ and CLS under 0.1. Eta: 1 day.
Bottom line: none of the five needs new content or new budget. Findings 1 to 4 are configuration in Duda, the Business Profile and DNS. Shipped before Oct 16, they turn opening-week press, reviews and the existing ads into rankings instead of letting them leak to name-alikes.
