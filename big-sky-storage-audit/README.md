# Big Sky Storage, SEO Audit (October 2026)

Technical, local, competitive and AI-visibility audit of **www.bigskystorage.com**, a new
heated and climate-controlled self storage facility in the Gallatin Canyon near Big Sky,
Montana, opening 16 October 2026.

**Live report:** https://lpgmarketing.github.io/SEO/big-sky-storage-audit/

## Headline

Google has indexed 3 of 18 sitemap URLs; none of the seven landing pages are linked from
the homepage. The Maps listing wins the pack for the brand but has no website or phone.
Every page's schema names the business "Looking for Storage". Brand rankings are still on
storagebigsky.com, and the bare domain serves an invalid certificate. Meanwhile, the
flagship AI models already name Big Sky Storage in 12 of 20 answers (13 counting a source cite).

## Method

GSC + URL Inspection API, GA4 Data API, Cubby BigQuery, Semrush, DataForSEO (Labs, SERP,
Lighthouse, backlinks, Google Ads volume), a full Playwright crawl of 16 URLs, Perplexity and
Brave research, and a 5 prompt x 4 model flagship LLM citation test with web search.

Next step: `/duda-sauce big-sky-storage` verifies every claim before building.
