Executive Summary

The situation in 60 seconds.

LookingForStorage.com is a thin, five-page umbrella site that ranks for almost nothing, is invisible to AI assistants, and carries a toxic backlink profile, while the individual facility websites it represents win the rankings and the AI citations outright. The right move is not to pour SEO into the portal so it competes with its own facilities. It is to reposition it as a clean brand and lead-routing hub, fix the trust-damaging errors on it, and disavow the spam.

  1. The portal is invisible and unmeasured. Semrush puts Authority Score at 2/100 with 0 estimated organic visits; DataForSEO sees ~34 visits/month, 97 of 100 keywords on page 3 or worse. It is not even verified in Google Search Console, while all 14 of LPG's other properties are. We cannot manage what we are not measuring.
  2. The facilities win; the umbrella does not. For "self storage gunnison," discountselfstorage.biz owns organic #4 with sitelinks plus local pack #2/#3; the portal is absent. discountselfstorage.biz alone pulls ~8,956 visits/month, roughly 60x the portal. The brand equity lives at the facility level.
  3. Zero AI citations for the brand, constant citations for the facilities. Across 20 flagship-LLM answers (Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT-5.5, Sonar-Reasoning-Pro), "LookingForStorage" was never recommended. The LPG facility sites were cited in 13 of 16 applicable cells, Moab Self Storage by all four models.
  4. The backlink profile is a liability, not an asset. ~20 of the top 30 referring domains sit on a single Moldova IP across throwaway .top/.icu/.xyz/.md domains with overt spam anchors. This is a PBN footprint that should be disavowed regardless of the strategic decision.
  5. The page-level errors are actively damaging trust. /contact ships a placeholder phone (123) 123-1234, the Colorado page has a "Smowmass" typo and a wrong Delta address, and the header phone (316-4664) disagrees with the About page (316-4644). These are credibility leaks on a brand asset.

Our verdict

This is a positioning problem disguised as an SEO problem. Chasing city head terms here would only cannibalize LPG's own facility sites, the assets that already rank and convert. Per Jim's standing "do not optimize" note on this domain, we agree the portal should not be a primary acquisition channel.

Instead: make it a credible, accurate brand hub that routes visitors to the right facility, captures only brand and operator-level category terms ("looking for storage," "leisure market storage operator"), passes link equity down to the facilities, and stops embarrassing the brand. Clean the spam, fix the errors, add it to GSC, ship basic schema. Low effort, real downside removed.

02 · The Five Critical Findings

What's actually
wrong with the portal.

Each was found by inspecting the live site or the search data, not by theory. Read together, they explain why a brand umbrella over a strong facility network is producing nothing, and in two cases is actively doing harm.

· · ·
1
CRITICAL · STRATEGY

The portal competes with its own facilities, and loses.

Impact: 149 keywords, 0 organic visits. The facilities win every market the portal lists.

Every "See Units" CTA on the Colorado page and every market nav item leaves the domain for an individual facility site (storagemoab.com, fridayharborstorage.com, discountselfstorage.biz, slateriverstorage.com, and 6 more). That is the correct routing. The problem is the portal still tries to rank for the same city terms its children own, from one thin /locations page, and loses to them every time. It adds no organic value and risks splitting brand signals.

Fix: Stop competing. Reposition the portal to target only brand and operator-level terms ("looking for storage," "leisure market self storage," "Ludlow Property Group storage") and route all city-intent traffic to the facility sites with clean internal links. Let the facility domains keep the city head terms. This is a content/IA decision, ~1 week.

2
CRITICAL · BACKLINKS

The link profile is a Moldovan PBN, not earned authority.

Impact: 746 links / 131 ref domains, but ~20 of the top 30 share one spam IP.

Semrush shows roughly 20 of the top 30 referring domains sitting on a single Moldova IP (195.20.19.178) across throwaway .top / .icu / .xyz / .md domains. Three unrelated domains (skitownstorage.com, storage4life.com, selfstorage4life.com) share the same anchor cloud, and overt spam anchors appear in the profile ("curious why lookingforstorage.com soared ... see on seoflox.com"). This is a private blog network footprint, the kind of profile that earns manual actions, not rankings.

Fix: Build a disavow file for the entire PBN cluster and submit it in Google Search Console (which first requires verifying the domain, see Finding 3). Stop any active link-buying on this domain. 1 day to assemble, do it regardless of the strategic decision.

3
CRITICAL · MEASUREMENT

The portal is not verified in Google Search Console.

Impact: zero first-party search data; cannot disavow, submit sitemaps, or see queries.

The LPG SEO service account holds siteFullUser on 14 properties, every other LPG domain including the facility sites for these very markets (storagemoab.com, fridayharborstorage.com, storagemoriches.com, discountselfstorage.biz). lookingforstorage.com is not one of them. A live pull returned 403 Forbidden. There is no query data, no index coverage report, and no way to file a disavow until this is fixed.

Fix: Add sc-domain:lookingforstorage.com as a domain property (captures www + apex + http/https) and grant the service account access. 15 minutes. Do this first; it unblocks the disavow and gives a real baseline for any decision.

4
CRITICAL · TRUST

Placeholder data and factual errors on a live brand asset.

Impact: a prospect on /contact sees a fake phone number. Credibility leak.

The crawl found, on live pages: /contact displays a placeholder phone (123) 123-1234 and promises a contact form that does not exist (0 forms on the page); /gunnison-valley has a heading typo "Smowmass" and lists the wrong address for Delta Hwy 50 (it shows "239 N 9th Street, Montrose," which is the MSS 9th Street address); and the site phone is inconsistent, header/footer (970) 316-4664 versus the About page's (970) 316-4644. Several outbound links to facility sites and Pay Rent use insecure http://.

Fix: Replace the placeholder phone with the real number and standardize it sitewide; build or remove the promised contact form; fix the "Smowmass" typo and the Delta address; upgrade all outbound links to https://. Half a day of edits. These are pure downside removal.

5
CRITICAL · ON-PAGE / SCHEMA

No H1s, bare titles, no descriptions, no business schema, orphan pages.

Impact: even brand-term ranking is handicapped; 5 pages are unreachable from nav.

The homepage and /locations have no H1; titles are bare ("Self Storage," "Colorado," "Locations"); 8 of 10 pages have no meta description; the homepage ships two duplicate WebSite JSON-LD blocks and there is no LocalBusiness / SelfStorage / Organization schema anywhere, nor any og:image. Five pages (/faq, /contact, /size-guide, /tenant-protection, /blog) are in the sitemap but orphaned from nav, including the only well-built page (the /faq, which has FAQPage schema). /blog has zero posts; /tenant-protection is a 56-word shell.

Fix: Add one real H1 per page, write unique titles + meta descriptions, deduplicate the WebSite schema and add a single Organization + per-location SelfStorage block (Section 06), link the orphan pages from the footer, and either fill or unpublish /blog and /tenant-protection. 2-3 days.

Bottom line: Findings 2, 3, 4 are pure risk removal you should do this week no matter what (disavow the PBN, verify in GSC, fix the fake phone and wrong address). Findings 1 and 5 are the repositioning: turn the portal into a clean hub that feeds the facilities instead of fighting them. None of this requires a big SEO budget, because the goal is not to make the portal rank for cities, it is to make it stop hurting and start helping.

Methodology

This audit combined five passes: (1) a live Playwright crawl of all 11 pages plus sitemap discovery, capturing H1-H6, meta, schema, word counts, alt-text, links, and 12 screenshots across desktop/tablet/mobile; (2) DataForSEO Labs (ranked keywords, SERP competitors, keyword ideas, Lighthouse, Google Trends, SERP checks); (3) Semrush (Authority Score, organic positions, backlinks, anchors, keyword gap, questions); (4) a Google Search Console access check via the LPG SEO service account; and (5) a 5x4 flagship-LLM citation test via DataForSEO's LLM API with web search enabled.

Models: claude-sonnet-4-6, gemini-3.1-pro-preview, gpt-5.5, sonar-reasoning-pro. Each prompt sent to all four with web_search=true, default temperature. 20 calls total, ~$1.05.

What we did NOT do / limitations
  • No GSC query data: the domain is not verified in Search Console, so all traffic figures are third-party estimates (Semrush ~0, DataForSEO ~34/mo) rather than first-party clicks.

  • Semrush Traffic Analytics + Site Audit unavailable on the current plan; Lighthouse + Playwright covered the technical layer instead.
  • Estimates are directional. Exact volumes/positions shift; the strategic conclusion (facilities win, umbrella is invisible) is robust across all sources.
