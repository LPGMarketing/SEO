Jimmy, in those markets "do more SEO" has almost nothing left to buy: the head terms get under 20-320 searches a month and we own them. Growth there is capture rate, reviews, and conversion.   • Conversion tracking finally works. GA4 key events across the portfolio: 181 → 559 (+209%) after the June conversion-rail work. For the first time the SEO program can be judged in rentals-adjacent actions, not clicks.
  • LLM search: we're already visible. In 23 live probes of GPT-5.5 and Gemini (web search on), LPG brands were mentioned in 19 of 20 market-model cells and were the outright recommendation in 10 - including a full three-brand sweep of Crested Butte. The models quote our own site copy back to users, so the June on-page work is literally the AEO payload. The fixable losses: brand-city mismatches (asked as Vail, Aspen, Center Moriches) and aggregators feeding models wrong prices for our units.

The honest verdict

This portfolio's SEO is a capture-rate game in small mountain markets, not a traffic-scale game. Total head-term demand across all 13 markets is roughly 2,400 searches a month - a single mid-size city keyword. We already dominate the map pack nearly everywhere; four markets are effectively maxed.

So the plan is not "more pages." It is: win Montrose organic, fix the two broken SERPs (South Padre, VAS-in-Eagle), convert the +25% impression gain into clicks with titles and internal links, keep pruning commodity clones, and turn our review lead into a moat. Where volume doesn't exist, the win is being the answer AI assistants and the pack give - and converting every searcher we already get.

Deep dives

Four things the topline numbers hide

1

Vail Airport is two different stories - and both are now good news

GSC: -45% clicks 28d, but last-7 +5% · DataForSEO: keywords 100→261, traffic value +221% Jan→Jun

Locally (Colorado queries), the May core update gutted the commodity blog clones - the cost-per-square-foot page alone lost ~89K impressions - and that bleed has now stopped: daily impressions have been flat at ~4,200-4,400 for three straight weeks and clicks are ticking up. Nationally, DataForSEO shows the opposite of a penalty: the domain's keyword footprint grew every month of 2026, with "prices of self storage units" (12,100/mo volume) at #3. The site was never "hit" - its weakest content was, which is exactly what we were deleting anyway.

Do: keep consolidating the remaining ~50 commodity guides (carefully, per-cluster - the batch approach already nearly 404'd two 1,000+ impression pages). Push the local money page /storage-gypsum-co (1,365 impressions at position 26) into the top 10 with internal links from the strong national pages and 3-4 Gypsum/Eagle local links. And fix the Eagle-proper local pack absence (finding in the Markets section).
2

We bought impressions in June; July's job is converting them to clicks

Ex-VAS: impressions +25.1%, clicks +1.5% · new pages sitting at positions 6-25

Every rebuilt site now has local pages indexed and collecting impressions - Estes' new rates/RV/boat pages (1,000+ combined impressions at positions 10-22), Discount's 5x5 guide (2,472 impressions, position 25), Snowmass' size guides (3,400+ impressions, positions 21-24), Montrose's drive-up cluster (positions 6-9). Positions 6-25 earn impressions; positions 1-3 earn the clicks. That's not failure - it's the normal first stop for new pages on low-authority domains. The lever now is CTR and rank nudging, not more pages.

Do: a portfolio-wide title/meta CTR pass on the ~20 highest-impression sub-top-3 pages (rates in the title, town name, "2026", review count where true); internal links from each site's strongest page to its money page; and the authority work in the Authority section. What decides the AI answer (from reading 23 transcripts)

  • Models quote our own site copy nearly verbatim - 24/7 access, paved drive-up, month-to-month, online move-in, rating badges. The on-page feature blocks and Key Takeaways ARE the AEO payload, and they demonstrably work.
  • Exact city-brand match wins. We win as "Moab Self Storage" in Moab and sweep Crested Butte; we lose when the question says "Vail" (brand says Gypsum/airport), "Aspen" (brand says Snowmass), or when in-town StorQuest beats out-of-town-named "Moriches Storage."
  • Review data feeds answers directly - models run "{brand} reviews" follow-up searches; GPT-5.5 flagged Friday Harbor's 9-review count as thin. Same lever as the local pack.
  • Aggregators own price intent and get our prices wrong - VAS was quoted at $489/mo from selfstorage.com. Wrong price, real damage, fully fixable by claiming listings and publishing from-prices.
  • Honest amenity disclosure routes demand cleanly - models correctly excluded Snowmass from climate queries and Moab from RV queries because the sites say so. Accuracy costs those intents but protects trust; the amenity-verification rule is paying off.
  • AI search volume on local terms is ~zero (only "storage units montrose co" registers, at 1/mo; "self storage" nationally is 15K and declining). The KPI is response share when someone does ask, not volume. DataForSEO's LLM-mentions corpus has zero coverage of our brands - live probing like this is the only way to measure, so it goes quarterly.

The LLM fix list

  • Claim and correct aggregator listings (SpareFoot, StorageCafe, RentCafe, selfstorage.com) with real from-prices for every facility - they are both a SERP presence and the LLMs' price source.
  • Bridge the city-brand gaps on-page: strengthen "Vail" association on VAS (the /top-5-vail asset already exists), "Aspen" pages on Snowmass (the /aspen-storage page is ranking - extend it), an "East Moriches / Center Moriches" page on Moriches.
  • Dedicated Gunnison location page for Discount - Gemini can't see it for "Gunnison" while GPT-5.5 ranks it #1; give crawlers an unambiguous Gunnison entity page.
  • Friday Harbor legacy cleanup: both models still surface "The Storage Place" at the same address - old citations need updating/redirects to finish the rebrand.
  • Keep review velocity climbing - it feeds pack AND AI answers.
  • Keep Key Takeaways + FAQPage schema rolling out to the remaining sites, verified facts only.

Method + caveats

How this was measured

  • GSC: all 16 verified properties via the openclaw-seo service account. Daily series Apr 1 – Jul 4; compare windows Jun 7 – Jul 4 vs May 10 – Jun 6 (both end-shifted 3 days for GSC lag); punch-list window Jun 30 – Jul 4 vs Jun 23-27, weekday-matched.
  • GA4: 15 properties, organic sessions + key events, same windows. Key-event totals include mid-window instrumentation rollout - treat the +209% as directional.
  • Semrush + DataForSEO Labs: authority, backlinks, monthly keyword/traffic-value trends Jan-Jun 2026. Their "June" rows largely predate the Jun 29-30 changes - the July rows (~Aug 15) are the confirmation checkpoint.
  • Live SERPs: 16 pulls on Jul 7 via DataForSEO, geo-located inside each market. GSC "average position" blends every searcher location; the live SERPs are the local ground truth where the two disagree (Estes being the example).
  • LLM probes: 23 questions to GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.5 Flash with web search enabled, Jul 7. Small-sample by nature; treated as directional and re-run quarterly.
  • Small-number noise: several brands run at single-digit daily clicks; weekly percentage swings under ~±50% on those are within noise. Verdicts triangulate at least three sources before calling better/worse.
  • Raw data: all pulls archived in the project repo under .tmp/seo-audit-2026-07/ (GSC/GA4 JSON, Semrush + DataForSEO per-domain files, SERP extracts, LLM transcripts).
